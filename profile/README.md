## Airdraft

Voice dictation for macOS. Hold a shortcut, speak, and insert text at your cursor.

- Run speech recognition locally or connect your own cloud API key.
- Refine transcripts with your choice of local or cloud language model.
- Customize writing profiles and vocabulary, and revisit past dictations.
